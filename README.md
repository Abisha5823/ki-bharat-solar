# KI Bharath Solar Energies – Business Website

## Live Project

Website: https://ki-bharat-solar.vercel.app

## Project Overview

KI Bharath Solar Energies required a professional digital platform to showcase its solar products, services, completed installations, and business information while enabling customers to connect with the company through online enquiry channels.

To address this requirement, I designed, developed, and deployed a multi-page business website with dynamic content management, structured product information, and customer enquiry capabilities.

## Business Problem

The client needed:

* A professional online presence
* Product and service showcase
* Project gallery to build customer trust
* Centralized company information
* Customer enquiry and lead generation system
* Mobile-friendly experience

## Solution

Developed and deployed a responsive business website that enables customers to:

* Explore solar products and solutions
* View completed installations and project galleries
* Access company information and services
* Submit enquiries and connect with the business

The website uses structured JSON-based content management, making it easy to maintain and update product, pricing, and company information.
